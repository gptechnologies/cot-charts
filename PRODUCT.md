# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Professional market participants and research-oriented users who need to inspect CFTC positioning and related foreign-exchange context quickly in a dense dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Present COT non-commercial positioning with clear time-series controls and a compact cross-currency summary. Success means users can compare positioning and relevant policy rates without leaving the dashboard or interpreting inconsistent data treatments.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, analytical, and direct. The interface should feel like a dependable research tool rather than a decorative consumer-finance product.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative fintech styling, gratuitous animation, marketing-page treatments, oversized metrics, and introducing a second visual or component vocabulary for small feature additions.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the established visual language and information density.
- Make data provenance and effective dates clear.
- Keep secondary data failures isolated from the primary COT workflow.
- Prefer small, legible additions over structural redesigns.
- Use familiar semantic controls and tables.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic table markup, keyboard and screen-reader compatibility, readable contrast, and indicators that do not depend on color alone. Do not introduce motion for static data updates.
